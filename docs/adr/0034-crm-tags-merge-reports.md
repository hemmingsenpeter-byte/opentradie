# ADR 0034: CRM tags, duplicate merge and customer reports

Status: Accepted (2026-10-24)

## Context

A sole tradie needs three everyday CRM tools: labelling customers (VIP, commercial, "no-marketing"), cleaning up the duplicate records that email intake and manual entry create, and seeing who brings in the money. The automations engine (ADR 0033) could not test customer labels because the data model had none.

## Decision

**Tags.** Migration `0023` adds `tags` (per tenant, name stored trimmed and lower-case and unique per tenant, colour from a fixed palette in `packages/database/src/check-values.ts` `TAG_COLOURS`) and `customer_tags` (composite FKs to customers and tags). Owner-only, audited, idempotent `POST /api/tags`, `PATCH|DELETE /api/tags/:id` (delete removes the tag from every customer) and `PUT /api/customers/:id/tags {tagIds}` (replaces the set); `GET /api/tags` and `GET /api/customers?tag=<name>` for viewers too. Customer responses carry `tags` and `mergedIntoId`. Automations gain the fact `customer.tags` (type `tags`; `contains` / `not_contains` / `exists`, whole-tag membership, case-insensitive). The three templates are unchanged; an owner can add `customer.tags not_contains "no-marketing"` to the review request themselves. Agents read tags through `customer.get` and may propose `customer.tag` (add/remove by name; owner approval always, never auto). Tags are in the per-customer and whole-business export; erasure deletes the customer's tag assignments but keeps the tags (business data).

**Duplicates.** `packages/core/src/crm.ts` scores pairs: exact normalised email (60), exact NZ phone after normalisation (spaces/punctuation dropped, `+64`, `0064`, `64` and `0` prefixes equivalent; 50), similar name (token match allowing a one-letter slip in tokens of 4+ letters, honorifics and accents ignored) at the same normalised address (40). A name alone is never reported. `GET /api/customers/duplicates` lists pairs not dismissed; `POST /api/customers/:id/not-duplicate {otherId}` records the ordered pair in `customer_duplicate_dismissals`.

**Merge.** `POST /api/customers/:id/merge {intoId, confirm}` (owner, the surviving customer's name typed) runs in the idempotency transaction. It locks both rows and refuses merging into itself, into an archived, erased or merged customer, merging an erased customer, merging an already-merged customer elsewhere, or a customer of another tenant (404). It finds every column with a foreign key to `customers` from the catalogue (`pg_constraint`), so tables added later are covered without a code change, and repoints them to the survivor. Special cases: properties with the same normalised address collapse onto the survivor's property (references repointed, the copy archived); tags are unioned; dismissals involving the merged customer are dropped. The merged customer row is kept, archived, with `merged_into_id` (CHECK: archived, not self); earlier merges into it are re-pointed so there are no chains. Append-only `job_events` and `audit_events` are not rewritten; readers follow `merged_into_id` (the automations engine does, and the PWA redirects an old customer link). `customer.merged` audit events record ids and counts only. `GET /api/customers/:id/merge-preview?into=` shows the counts that would move and warnings.

**Accounting.** OPENTRADIE does not merge contacts in Xero. The merged customer's `external_references` contact mapping and its posted invoices stay as they are; the preview warns (`ACCOUNTING_CONTACT_NOT_MERGED`, `POSTED_INVOICES`) and the owner merges the contacts in Xero themselves.

**Reports.** `GET /api/reports/customers?period=this_month|last_month|this_financial_year` or `?from&to` (inclusive dates, business time zone; NZ financial year 1 April to 31 March), `&format=csv`, for owner and viewer. Per customer: jobs (not cancelled) created in the period, quotes sent in the period and accepted (win rate in basis points), invoiced excl./GST/incl. from invoices issued in the period with status approved, posting, posted, sync_failed, part_paid or paid (drafts and void excluded), paid (latest payment snapshot, or the total for a `paid` invoice without one), outstanding, first and last job date, repeat flag (jobs on two or more distinct days up to the period end). Summary: totals, top 5 by revenue, new customers (first ever job in the period) and repeat customers, overall win rate, average days from issue date to the first fully-paid snapshot. All arithmetic is integer cents in `computeCustomerReport`; CSV renders dollars from cents and neutralises spreadsheet formulas.

## Consequences

- The merge is generic over customer references; a new table with a non-trivial unique constraint on its customer column would need a special case (the integration test sweeps `information_schema` after a merge).
- Paid dates come from payment snapshots, which only exist where reconciliation runs (fake adapter in CI); average days to pay is empty otherwise.
- Status: Implemented and tested (`packages/core/src/crm.test.ts`, `tests/integration/crm.test.ts`, `apps/web/src/Customers.test.tsx`, `tests/e2e/zz-crm.spec.ts`). Xero contact merging is not implemented.
