## Put it on your phone

- **Android:** open it in Chrome → tap ⋮ → **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).

## Turn an enquiry into a quote

1. Open **Assistant** and type what the customer wants, the way you'd say it. For example: _"Mrs Johnson needs two power points replaced, check her details and prepare a quote."_
2. If there's more than one customer with that name, it asks which one you mean. Tap the right one.
3. It shows a **draft quote** using **your** prices, plus anything it needs you to check (**missing information**) and the assumptions it made.
4. Check it, then tap **Approve** or **Reject**. Nothing is approved without you.

The AI suggests jobs and picks prices from your price list, but your price list sets every amount. It never makes up a price. If a price is missing, it tells you instead of guessing.

## Keep your account safe

- **Change your password:** Settings › Security › Change password. Every other phone or computer you were signed in on is signed out.
- **Turn on two-factor sign-in:** Settings › Security › Set up two-factor. Add the key shown to an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, 1Password, Aegis…) by typing or pasting it (there is no QR code yet), then enter the 6-digit code. Save the 10 recovery codes somewhere safe: each one signs you in once if you lose your phone.
- **See where you're signed in:** Settings › Security › Signed-in devices. Sign out anything you don't recognise.
