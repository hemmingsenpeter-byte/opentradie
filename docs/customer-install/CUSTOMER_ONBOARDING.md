# Getting ready for your OPENTRADIE setup

Thanks for choosing OPENTRADIE. We'll set everything up for you on a short video call (about 45 minutes). Please have these ready before the call.

## Things to have on hand

- [ ] **Your business details:** business name, your trade, whether you're GST registered, and the email address you want to log in with.
- [ ] **Your prices:** fill in the rates sheet we sent (`rates-template.csv`; it opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets). Use your real prices. If you don't charge for something, delete that row. We will never make up a price for you. After setup you can change prices yourself in the app under **Settings › Price list**, or export the sheet, edit it and import it again.
- [ ] **A credit or debit card** for two online accounts that you will own: one for hosting, one for the AI. You pay those companies directly, and we never see your card details.
- [ ] **A computer** (not just a phone) for the call, so you can share your screen.
- [ ] **Signed agreements:** the licence and service agreement we sent.

## What happens on the call

1. You create two accounts while we guide you. You do the typing.
   - **Render**, which hosts your app and your data.
   - **OpenRouter**, which provides the AI. You set a monthly spending limit, so it can never cost more than you choose.
2. You add us to your Render account so we can set things up.
3. We build your app and load your business details and prices.
4. We test it together with a pretend customer enquiry.
5. You set your own password. Nobody else, including us, will know it.
6. We remove our access. From then on, the app and the accounts are entirely yours.

## What it costs you to run

You pay Render (hosting) and OpenRouter (AI) directly each month. We'll show you current prices on the call. You can see your bills and change your limits in those accounts at any time.
